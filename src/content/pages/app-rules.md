---
title: App rules
description: What you can expect from apps distributed through the App Fair.
---

Apps distributed through the App Fair must meet these rules. They apply to new apps and updates. Developers can find the submission requirements in the [inclusion criteria](/docs/inclusion-criteria/).

## Always free

App Fair apps are free to download and use. No in-app purchases, subscriptions to unlock app features, or paid upgrades are permitted.

## No advertising

Apps contain no ads or advertising SDKs. Their purpose is to provide the app’s features, not to sell your attention.

## No analytics or tracking

Apps contain no analytics, tracking or telemetry SDKs, and do not collect usage data for those purposes. Network access and permissions must serve a stated app feature. The listing and privacy information must explain what data the app uses and where it goes.

## Source you can inspect

The app’s source code and release history are public. You can inspect how it works, review changes and report problems. Apps use the AGPL-3.0-only license with the App Fair Distribution Exception; included libraries must also be free software. Closed-source components cannot be bundled into an app.

## Review before publication

The App Fair reviews each submitted release and builds it from the identified source commit before publishing it. Store review is a separate step. Public source and review make problems easier to investigate; they do not guarantee that an app has no defects.

## Report a problem

Use the app’s support link to report bugs. If an app appears to violate these rules, [contact the App Fair](mailto:support@appfair.org) with the app name, version and a description of the problem.

These rules may change as the project develops. See the [developer inclusion criteria](/docs/inclusion-criteria/) for the current requirements.
