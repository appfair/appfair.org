The App Fair is a free and open source app marketplace. These policies apply to its users and developers across supported platforms. The marketplace operator is APP FAIR, 2 rue Nouvelle de Wailly, 63000 Clermont-Ferrand, France; see the [imprint](/imprint/) for project organizations.

## Terms of service

Use the marketplace lawfully and respect the [app rules](/app-rules/) and [Trust & Safety Policy](/marketplace/security/). Do not misuse the service or interfere with other users, apps or security controls. Each app remains subject to its own license and applicable privacy information. App availability and supported features may change; access or distribution may be restricted for policy, security or legal reasons. Nothing in these terms limits rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Developers participate under the [Developer Agreement / Terms of Distribution](/marketplace/distribution/) and [inclusion criteria](/docs/inclusion-criteria/). Submission is open to developers who meet those requirements; approval is based on the app and its compliance with the published rules.

## Privacy policy

The [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) explains information processed for users and developers, its purposes, sharing, retention and available controls. It also sets out the privacy information required for apps. Contact [privacy@appfair.net](mailto:privacy@appfair.net) for privacy requests.

## Trust & safety policy

The [Trust & Safety Policy](/marketplace/security/) defines prohibited content and behavior, review and enforcement, and reporting channels. The App Fair prohibits malware, fraud, illegal and exploitative content, infringement and privacy violations. Report concerns to [safety@appfair.net](mailto:safety@appfair.net).

## Customer support

For direct help with the marketplace, installations, updates, an app listing or a complaint, email [support@appfair.net](mailto:support@appfair.net). Include the app name, platform, version and a description of the issue. We provide timely responses appropriate to the nature and urgency of the request. For app-specific bugs, the developer’s support link may also help.

Apps are free to download and use. For a payment, donation, disputed charge or refund question connected with the App Fair, contact the same support address with the relevant reference, without payment-card details. We can address transactions we handle or direct you to the responsible provider. For transactions through another provider, that provider’s support and refund processes may apply.
