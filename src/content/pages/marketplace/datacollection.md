The [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) covers the App Fair websites and marketplace, including data supplied by users and developers, service-provider processing, retention and privacy requests.

Developers must provide the [app privacy information](/privacy/#developers) required for every listing. Apps must meet the [app rules](/app-rules/); a privacy policy or consent cannot authorize prohibited analytics, tracking or telemetry.

For questions or requests concerning personal information, contact [privacy@appfair.net](mailto:privacy@appfair.net).
