# Site style guide

## Project name

Use **the App Fair** or **the App Fair Project** when referring to the project as an
entity. Include the article when the name is a subject, object, or possessive.
Capitalize **The** at the start of a sentence or a standalone label; use **the** within
a sentence. Apply this rule to prose, headings, descriptions, link text, diagrams and
accessibility labels.

Examples:

- “The App Fair does not require exclusivity.”
- “Submit the release to the App Fair.”
- “The workflow uses the App Fair’s developer accounts.”
- “Approval by the App Fair is required.”
- “Build and publish with the App Fair.”

Keep ordinary adjectival uses, such as **an App Fair app**, **App Fair apps**, and
**an App Fair maintainer**. For review or publication by the project, prefer
**review by the App Fair** or **distribution through the App Fair** when that makes
the actor clearer.

Preserve exact product and license names, including **App Fair Publisher** and
**App Fair Distribution Exception**. The short logo wordmark **App Fair** may stand
alone. Do not alter registered legal names, verbatim quotations, transcripts, external
publication titles, code identifiers, commands or URLs to apply this rule.

For translations, use the target language’s grammar; do not insert the English word
“the” into translated prose.

## Documentation

Use short, direct answers and concrete instructions. Keep commands copyable, distinguish
optional tools from required steps, and link to the detailed procedure instead of
repeating it. Preserve explicit FAQ anchor IDs when revising question text.
